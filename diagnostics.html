Model validation

Reviews that compare scoring outputs against benchmarks, parity targets, and drift bounds.
  • Benchmark Review 10-day benchmark performance vs main25 / wl25 baselines and concentration warnings.
  • Scoring Parity Review Production scoring vs the parity reference across the universe.
  • Low-Risk Drift Review Detects drift in the low-risk sub-score relative to historical distributions.
  • Ranking Diagnostics Full per-ticker diagnostic dump of ranking components.
  • Rank vs Close Tracker Rolling daily snapshots of rank and close-change per ticker (dynamic universe, 10-snapshot window).

Signals

Diagnostic signal overlays that feed bounded ACT / GO / ACC adjustments on top of base AI.
  • Pine Go/No-Go Diagnostic Pine v6 gate stack — GO / WAIT / WEAK signal and contribution to the AI score.
  • Cool-off Cohort Tracking Tracks cohorts coming out of WAIT cool-off and post-blocker recovery.
  • Accumulation Signal Meter Composite accumulation indicator (rel-vol + MFI + bar strength + RSI zone).
  • Continuity Score Diagnostic 0–100 trend-continuity score (rel strength, close location, volume support, MA distance, reversal health) with 7-snapshot tracking. Does not affect production rank.
  • Swing 2-5D Score Diagnostic 0–100 short-horizon (2-5 day) positive-swing score — TECH trend, momentum thrust, continuity, accumulation, GO gate, liquidity, sentiment/catalyst, with overextension/cool-off/earnings/low-liquidity penalties. Surfaced as the compact 2-5D badge on the main & watchlist tables. JSON artifact (includes a missing-data roadmap); does not affect production rank and is separate from the production SWING score.
  • Activity-Adjusted Review Activity overlay (ACT) — liquidity + rel-vol + Pine overlay applied to AI score.

External benchmarks

Compares internal scoring against third-party signals to surface confirmation or disagreement.
  • External Benchmark Review e-trade, Fidelity, MarketBeat, TradingView, Zacks vs internal ranking.
  • Disagreement Queue Review Tickers where external sources disagree strongly with the internal model.

Planning & closeout

Roadmap and closeout tracking for the project.
  • Project Closeout Roadmap Remaining work and exit criteria for the project.
