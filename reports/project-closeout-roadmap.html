Manual review steps for the next session

  1. Open the dashboard and click into Activity-adjusted. Compare the top-25 entrants and drops. Note any clearly-wrong activity-adjusted entries (e.g. earnings within 7d, low-quality momentum).
  2. Open Disagreement review. Optionally download the CSV (link at the top) and edit the starter template data/reports/disagreement_review_state_template.csv offline. For each severe row, decide keep / watchlist_only / ignore / needs_more_data.
  3. Persist decisions: edit data/reports/disagreement_review_state.json (the canonical state) to record reviewed=true, decision, and notes for each reviewed entry. The next run will preserve those edits.
  4. Open Accumulation meter. Cross-reference the top-25 with Pine clean-go and activity-adjusted top-25 (overlap sections are inline). Names that appear in all three are the highest-conviction diagnostic candidates.
  5. Open Pine go/no-go and cool-off cohorts. Verify no new overextended_bb blockers on names you decided to keep.
