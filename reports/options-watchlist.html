Pick 2: Meta Platforms (META) — Conviction Level: 5/5

Closing Price (April 27): $678.62[^27][^28]

Meta is the single highest-conviction earnings catalyst in this report. Q1 2026 earnings are scheduled April 29 after close, with analysts expecting EPS of ~$6.66–$7.10 and revenue of $55.36 billion — approximately 31% year-over-year growth. Meta beat EPS estimates in Q1, Q2, and Q4 2025. Q4 2025 EPS came in at $8.88 versus the $8.19 estimate — an 8.42% beat. The critical IV insight: options are pricing a 6.24% implied move into the earnings print, but META has historically moved an average of 10.65% in either direction post-earnings over the last four quarters. This means the market is underpricing volatility relative to recent history — a favorable setup for call buyers. META's total OI stands at 2.8 million contracts, above its 52-week average at the 83.5th percentile. Short interest is just 1.21% of float.[^29][^30][^4][^18][^31][^32][^14]

Momentum Indicators: - Earnings Surprise Probability: +5.76% (analysts revised upward recently)[^2] - Call/put OI ratio: 0.66 — firmly bullish skew[^33] - IV priced at 6.24% move vs. 10.65% actual average moves — IV underpriced[^18] - META OI at 83.5th percentile — unusually high institutional demand[^32] - Revenue growth expected at ~31% YoY at $55.36B scale[^4]

General Risk Disclosure

Options positions can lose 100% of invested capital rapidly. All calls lose value through theta decay if the underlying does not move. Post-earnings IV crush is a real and certain risk for META and AMZN if held through the April 29 earnings announcement — the implied volatility premium collapses after the report regardless of stock direction. Consider sizing each position at 1–3% of available trading capital. Use limit orders on entry to avoid overpaying on wide bid/ask spreads. The May 15/22 monthly expirations are selected deliberately over weekly options to reduce gamma and theta exposure, providing a wider window for the thesis to develop.
