## Notes on Fidelity Research Page Structure

- **URL pattern:** `https://digital.fidelity.com/prgw/digital/research/quote/dashboard/ratings-sentiment?symbol=TICKER`
- **Equity Summary Score (ESS):** Provided by LSEG StarMine. Scale 0–10. Color-coded badge: dark red = Very Bearish (0–2), red = Bearish (2–4), yellow/gray = Neutral (4–6), green = Bullish (6–8), bright green = Very Bullish (8–10).
- **Provider table columns:** Independent Firm Name | LSEG StarMine Relative Accuracy (numeric weight) | Standardized Opinion (Buy/Sell/Neutral/Outperform/Underperform with colored dot) | Opinion Date
- **1-Year History chart:** Based on "standardized opinions" provided by Investars; shows distribution across Very Bullish / Bullish / Neutral / Bearish / Very Bearish over time.
- **Sentiment tab** (separate URL: `.../analysis?symbol=TICKER`): Contains S&P Global Market Intelligence fundamental factors (Valuation, Quality, Growth, Financial Health), Trading Central technical sentiment (Short/Mid/Long-term), MSCI ESG scores, Context Analytics S-Score.
- **Price targets:** Not prominently visible on the Analyst Ratings tab; may require clicking individual provider reports.
- **Tickers with no coverage:** XE had no analyst rating or reports on Fidelity.
